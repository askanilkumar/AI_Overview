    Your learning path — the same journey, on foot
    
  
    Learn it in the order you just flew it. Part A walks down the AI family (10,000 ft). Part B builds the five Gen AI layers (5,000 ft) — ending with the exact support bot you watched at 1,000 ft. Every phase ends with a project.
    

  
    
    
    Part A · Understand — down the AI family
    

    
    
      
    Before you take off
    Foundations — Python, data & a little maths
    ~4–6 weeks
    
      
    Python basics, working with tables in pandas and SQL, and just enough maths — averages, probability and vectors. Everything after this stands on it.
    
      
    PythonJupyterpandasSQLGit & GitHub
    
      
    Build: analyse a sales or expenses CSV and chart the top items.
    
    
    

    
    
      
    Machine Learning
    Teach a machine with examples
    ~4–6 weeks
    
      
    Supervised vs unsupervised learning, regression and classification, train/test split, and how to measure a model (accuracy, precision, recall). Learn to think: data → model → prediction.
    
      
    scikit-learnmatplotlibKaggle
    
      
    Build: a spam-message classifier, or a house-price predictor.
    
    
    

    
    
      
    Deep Learning
    Neural networks & how they learn
    ~4 weeks
    
      
    Neurons and layers, how training works (intuition first, not heavy maths), CNNs for images, embeddings — and the idea of attention behind Transformers.
    
      
    PyTorchGoogle ColabHugging Face
    
      
    Build: an image classifier using a pre-trained model (transfer learning).
    
    
    

    
    
      
    Generative AI
    LLMs, prompts & embeddings
    ~3 weeks
    
      
    How LLMs work — tokens, context window, temperature. Call a model through an API, learn prompting (system prompts, few-shot examples, JSON output) and turn text into embeddings.
    
      
    Claude APIOpenAI APIGemini APIOllama
    
      
    Build: a resume reviewer that scores a CV and suggests improvements.

    Key takeaway
    
    AI ⊃ Machine Learning ⊃ Deep Learning ⊃ Generative AI. A Gen AI system is inputs → five layers → outputs. Understand it from the top down — then build it from the ground up, one layer at a time.
